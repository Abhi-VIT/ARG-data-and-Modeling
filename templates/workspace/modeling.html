Phase 03 / Build a model

Machine learning

Select a task, choose features, and fit a model. Results stay tied to this revision.

Preprocessing is fitted on training rows only. Random splitting assumes independent observations.

Hyperparameters

Check parameters to tune. Enter candidate lists such as [0.1, 1, 10] or ["sqrt", "log2"]. Up to 20 candidate models.

Training runs in the background. Numeric gaps are imputed and categories encoded within the pipeline. Large designs are capped with a clear error.
